Day by day

Revenue is routes × rate per route, plus any extra income. Extra payments you make count as a cost. Driver wages, van hire and insurance are 4-weekly, so they're not entered here. Leave a card blank to skip that FD. Clearing a saved card removes it.

–

4-week period

–

Driver wages, van hire and insurance are charged once per 4-week period, whatever the number of days worked. Each active FD is charged its usual 4-weekly amounts (set under FDs & rates). Enter each period's actual wages here, change any other figure for just this period, then save. Saving also fixes this period's figures, so later changes to an FD's usual amount won't alter it.

FDs

Each FD is linked to an email address and a depot. The rate is filled in on the daily entry screen. Driver wages, van hire and insurance are the usual 4-weekly amounts, charged automatically each period. Wages can be changed for each period on the Wages, van & insurance tab.

Add an FD

Moving an FD to the other depot only affects new entries. Past days stay with the depot they were worked at.

Depot rates and period

Any period start works, past or future. Weeks run 7 days from it, and every period runs 28 days, then resets. New rates apply to days you enter after saving; saved days keep their rate.
